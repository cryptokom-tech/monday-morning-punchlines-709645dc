Entry for the [Monday Morning Punchlines](https://make.fun/c/u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters, judged on laughs.

## The joke

> Monday morning: my bed filed for full custody of me — and honestly, its case is stronger than mine.

99 characters. A custody battle with a bed: the setup sounds like a breakup, the punchline lands twice — a legal "case", and the bed's case being stronger than mine.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no environment reads, no dependencies.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
